# FIRE Calc

A command-line calculator for the FIRE movement: savings rate, the 4% rule,
and a realistic withdrawal schedule across a long horizon.

## What it does
- Computes how fast you can reach financial independence from a savings rate
- Stresses the 4% rule over a 40-50 year horizon, not just one snapshot
- Builds a stage-based spending schedule instead of one flat withdrawal rate
- Answers "can I retire early on a 65k salary?" with real take-home math

## The thinking behind it
The numbers are backed by the practical guides on [FIREnomics](https://firenomics.com/)
- savings rate, index funds, the 4% rule, and withdrawal strategy.
